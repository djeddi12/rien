# Opportunity Engine

Autonomous commercial opportunity intelligence.

This private project is designed to discover commercially valuable opportunities, score them with deterministic evidence, match them to monetizable offers, generate useful digital assets, measure outcomes, and learn from real conversion data.

## Core loop

Internet signals → demand discovery → opportunity detection → commercial scoring → offer matching → asset strategy → execution → traffic/clicks/conversions → learning → next opportunity.

## Non-negotiable principles

1. Evidence first. Never invent demand, rankings, traffic, commissions, or conversion data.
2. Deterministic systems measure facts; Qwen interprets, explains, and writes.
3. Every opportunity gets a score and an explicit reason for acceptance/rejection.
4. Content is not the product by default. A winning opportunity may produce a page, calculator, comparison, workflow, video brief, or a combination.
5. Real revenue/conversion data becomes training memory for future decisions.
6. Human approval is required before irreversible external actions in the first versions.
7. FastSEOHub remains a separate product and codebase.

## Initial opportunity score

- Demand: 25%
- Commercial intent: 20%
- Competition: 15%
- Affiliate/monetization value: 15%
- Content gap: 10%
- SERP opportunity: 10%
- Execution cost: 5%

The scoring package will normalize all dimensions to 0–100 and expose both the total score and component evidence.

## Planned modules

- discovery: demand and market signal collectors
- scoring: deterministic opportunity scoring
- offers: affiliate/product intelligence
- strategy: asset and execution planning
- qwen: controlled AI interpretation/writing layer
- analytics: clicks, conversions, revenue and attribution
- learning: opportunity memory and outcome feedback
- dashboard: operator control plane

## Safety

No automated claim should be published as fact without a source. No affiliate program terms should be treated as permanent; current terms must be verified before promotion.
